A day at Promethia

How a session works

Twice a week, every session follows a simple rhythm, so kids know what to expect and can focus on the fun part.

  1. 1

    Arrive & team up

    Students join their cohort and grab their project kit.

  2. 2

    Explain 5 min

    A mentor introduces one skill with a real example.

  3. 3

    Play & build most of it

    Students learn the skill through games, challenges and team builds. Promo nudges with questions, never answers.

  4. 4

    Reflect 5 min

    Everyone shares what they learned and what they'd try next.

  5. ★

    Demo day (every so often)

    Students present their work to families and friends.

Parent FAQ

Is it safe?

Yes. Sessions are in person with trainers present the whole time, and run in partnership with schools.

Is it really offline?

Yes. Kids learn with their hands and with each other. Screens are used only when a project needs them, for example to understand how AI works.

What will my child actually build?

Prototypes, pitches, research projects, designs and short films, presented at demo days.

Which grades can join?

Right now Grades 5–12 in Bangalore. We plan to open to all grades over time.

How often are sessions?

Twice a week, in person.
