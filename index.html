What is Promethia?

An offline, school-partnered cohort program where students learn by doing. No screens-first lectures: kids work with their hands, in teams, on projects they care about.

We start with Grades 5–12 in Bangalore and plan to grow to every grade. Across every domain, students practise design thinking, public speaking and show their work at demo days.

  • 2×sessions a week, in person
  • 5core domains to explore
  • 100%hands-on, offline learning

Five domains. Endless builds.

🛠️

Making & Engineering

How a session works

Every session follows the same simple rhythm, so kids know what to expect and can focus on the fun part.

  1. 1

    Explain

    5 min

    A mentor introduces one skill with a real example.

  2. 2

    Play

    Most of the session

    Students learn it through games, challenges and team builds.

  3. 3

    Reflect

    5 min

    Everyone shares what they learned and what they'd try next.

For parents

Your child builds confidence by making real things and talking about them. You'll always know what they're learning.

  • Small, mentored cohorts in a safe, in-person setting
  • Skills that last: problem solving, teamwork, communication
  • A private portfolio showing your child's progress
  • Promo, our flame guide, encourages kids to think, never hands over answers

For schools

We partner with schools to run Promethia cohorts on campus or nearby. We bring the curriculum, mentors and materials; you bring curious students.

  • Structured twice-weekly program that fits your timetable
  • Project work that complements classroom learning
  • Demo days your whole community can attend
  • Clear progress reporting for students and parents
